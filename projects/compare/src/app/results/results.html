Automated scan

@ngbracket/a11y-devtools ran axe {{ axeVersion }} on both pages in light and dark themes on {{ scanDate }}. The dark pass lists only problems that light doesn't already have, and found none on either page. It also ran its keyboard checks, which look for controls that Tab can't reach and for focus traps. In the screenshots the overlay is on: each label is a finding, named after the component that rendered it, and the numbered line is the Tab order.
